# The Cleaner

An iPhone shooter. A man in a suit walks a rain-soaked city block at night with a Glock while attackers rush him from both sides.

## Play on iPhone
1. Open the game's page in Safari on your iPhone.
2. Turn the phone sideways. The game is landscape-only.
3. For full screen, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, then launch it from the home-screen icon.

On a computer, the page just asks you to open it on an iPhone.

## Controls
- **Tap an attacker:** shoot him. The shot snaps to the head, chest or knee nearest your finger.
- **◀ / ▶ (hold):** walk back / hurry forward
- **⟳:** reload the 17-round magazine. It also reloads by itself when empty.
- **II:** pause

## How it plays
- Every third attacker triggers **slow motion** when hit. The world turns black-and-white and the camera zooms in on each bullet impact. Keep tapping him (up to 5 hits) and watch the bullets fly and the blood spray.
- Everyone else drops from a single shot, and how they fall depends on where you hit them:
  - **Head:** dies instantly and flies backward
  - **Chest:** drops straight down
  - **Knee:** falls forward onto his face
- Gunmen show up later. A red laser sight warns you before each shot.
- Finishing a slow-motion kill restores 1 HP.
