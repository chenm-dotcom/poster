## Pony Ketamine

`pony-ketamine/` — take a selfie or upload a photo and an AI stylist (Higgsfield) gives you long hair with super-short blunt micro bangs in your own hair colour, with a goth ta-da and a soft techno beat. Runs on Cloudflare Workers with a 400-use cap. See `pony-ketamine/SETUP.md` to go live.
